## Context

What is the issue or forcing function that motivates this decision? Describe
the system context, constraints, and stakes. Prefer prose over bullets.
Reference external docs or prior ADRs where relevant. Capture the state of
the world *at the time of the decision*; do not rewrite this section later.

## Decision

State the decision clearly and in the active voice. One or two paragraphs.
"We will ..." is the right register.

## Alternatives considered

List the realistic alternatives that were weighed, each with a one-paragraph
summary of why it was rejected. Rejecting "do nothing" explicitly is often
useful.

## Consequences

Enumerate the positive, negative, and neutral outcomes of this decision.
Include second-order effects (what this forces elsewhere in the system).
Separate "we accept" (known costs) from "we assume" (bets that may fail).

## Revisit trigger

What signal, event, or condition should cause us to re-open this decision?
This is *not* a calendar reminder; it is a concrete observable (e.g.
"onboarding new engineers takes > 4 weeks to first merged PR", not "in six
months"). If the trigger fires, we open a follow-up ADR; we do not quietly
drift.
